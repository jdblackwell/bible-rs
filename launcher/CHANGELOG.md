# Omalauncher Preview 1 — draft

**Bible for Omalauncher — based on bible-rs**

Based on Micky's bible-rs and its contributors, starting from ForrestKnight's
fork at `67aef578cc9d6065b588b48d789be3d4d2c68685`. Adaptation and testing: Javahax.

- Phone-sized onboarding with reachable controls and scrolling.
- Touch scrolling and verse/word selection without Shift.
- Settings open in the current phone window.
- Notes and bookmarks use phone layouts, with previews, navigation and deletion.
- Tapping the exposed content closes the sidebar after choosing a section.
- Phone sidebar transitions avoid repeated chapter typesetting; note edits are
  batched and flushed on navigation and normal exit.
- Optional OWL 11 widget with offline reading, search, saved passages and bridge.
- Visible original-project credits, build identity, license bundle, portable
  installer, installation backups, uninstall manifest and package checksums.

This is a community preview, not an official upstream or standalone Android
release. See README.md for installation and outstanding testing/limitations.
